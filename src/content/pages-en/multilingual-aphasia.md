Aphasia in a multilingual person poses particular challenges for care. Each language can be affected differently, and languages may recover at different rates.

This part of my clinical and academic work is grounded in a perspective that values linguistic and cultural diversity. It aims to offer assessment and intervention approaches that are better suited to multilingual people living with aphasia, drawing on the available evidence.

<section class="tool-group">

### Other resources


<details class="accordion">
<summary>Aphasia Therapy Finder<br/><a href="https://aphasiatherapyfinder.com/"><em>view online</em></a></summary>

Aphasia Therapy Finder is a searchable database of evidence-based aphasia therapies. You need to create an account to use it – it's *free*. It offers easy access to therapy descriptions, evidence summaries and resources for quickly putting evidence-based therapies into clinical practice.  
Note: the therapies are not necessarily multilingual; most are in English.

</details>

<details class="accordion">
<summary>Multilingual Topics in Communication Sciences &amp; Disorders (MultiCSD)<br/><a href="https://sites.google.com/view/multicsd"><em>view online</em></a></summary>

A website with information about the structure of [*different languages*](https://sites.google.com/view/multicsd/global-languages?authuser=0) and [*different cultures*](https://sites.google.com/view/multicsd/global-cultures?authuser=0), along with advice and [*clinical examples*](https://sites.google.com/view/multicsd/multilingualism-multiculturalism/working-with-cld-clients/adults/multilingual-considerations-for-aphasia?authuser=0).

</details>

<details class="accordion">
<summary>AI assistant for assessing and managing multilingual aphasia 🤖<br/><a href="https://chatgpt.com/g/g-688958ba06108191897dfa67d68031d8-multilingual-aphasia-slp-assistant"><em>view online</em></a></summary>

This GPT is a specialized assistant designed to support speech-language pathologists working with multilingual people living with aphasia, particularly after a stroke. It draws on a carefully selected knowledge base of more than 80 scientific publications, case studies and clinical guidelines to provide evidence-based answers.
