My publications reflect my commitment to understanding acquired communication disorders and improving care for the people who live with them. Through my research I explore themes such as intervention effectiveness, underlying neurofunctional mechanisms, and the effects of therapy on neuroplasticity. Below is a selection of my work published in peer-reviewed journals.
