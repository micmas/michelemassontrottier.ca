## CALMaR: automated brain lesion mapping and reporting

*Co-designed, Automated Lesion Mapping and Reporting* · since 2026

CALMaR is an open-source pipeline that automatically segments stroke lesions on structural MRI, checks the quality of the results and produces a report that clinicians can interpret. It is being co-designed with the Metro North Health speech pathology team and people with lived experience of aphasia, to provide a privacy-first decision-support tool that helps guide intervention after stroke. The project is funded by a UQ EAIT Early Career Philanthropic Grant.

## Neurodesk: open and reproducible neuroimaging

Core contributor · since 2025

[Neurodesk](https://www.neurodesk.org/) is an open-source platform that packages more than 90 neuroimaging tools in containers. It runs on a laptop, on a computing cluster or directly in the browser. My work focuses on clinical translation, training and publishing reproducible analyses. Neurodesk received the 2025 AIIA iAward for Technology Platform of the Year.

## WAGademy: a working accountability group for research students

*Working Accountability Group* · School of Electrical Engineering and Computer Science, UQ

WAGademy gives higher degree by research students three hours of protected, timed work alongside others doing the same, often preceded by a short themed discussion. The format, tools and a library of topics are documented online so that anyone can start their own group.
