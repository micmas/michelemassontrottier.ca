Teaching in speech-language pathology has allowed me to share my knowledge with students in the field. My teaching draws on the latest scientific and clinical advances, with an emphasis on learning through practice and critical reflection.

I had the opportunity to contribute to the redesign of the speech-language pathology program at the Université de Montréal, bringing in new perspectives based on recent research. My experience also includes creating courses, from designing the syllabus to developing content suited to students' needs.

### **Supervision and mentoring:**

I have also supervised students at every level (bachelor's, master's and doctoral) through research projects, clinical placements and mentoring programs. My goal is to help each student develop their clinical and academic skills in a collaborative environment.
