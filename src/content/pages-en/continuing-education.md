I offer continuing education tailored to the needs of speech-language pathology professionals, based on the latest research and clinical practice. These courses aim to strengthen clinicians' skills and bring scientific advances into everyday intervention.

My courses are currently offered in French through the Association québécoise des orthophonistes et audiologistes (AQOA); course titles are shown in French with an English translation.

## Teaching approach

My courses take an interactive, hands-on approach that encourages collaborative learning and sharing experience. I focus on putting knowledge into practice, so that every participant leaves with tools they can use directly in their clinical work.

### Topics

- Effectiveness of speech-language interventions
- Neurocognitive disorders and communication
- Using neuroscience in clinical intervention
- Evidence-based approaches
- New technologies and artificial intelligence in speech-language pathology
